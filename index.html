Vietnam’s 54 ethnic groups

Circle area represents the 2024 population estimate. Each circle is centered on the administrative city of the province or municipality with the largest recorded population of that group in 2019.

Locations use 2019 administrative boundaries. A city marker represents its wider census province or municipality. Dark locator dots let you select a city even when its population circles are very small or overlap. Select a group in the list to highlight its circle.
